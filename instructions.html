Instructions

Whack-a-Mole - PMF edition

Game goal

Collect as many points as possible in 30 seconds. Click on "exam" and bonus icons that pop up in the grid. Watch out for the negative ones!

Scoring

Final exam: +20pts
Project: +10pts
Test: +5pts
Bonus: +50pts
Bonus: +5s
Bug: -30pts
Reset! Watch out!

Each icon stays briefly on the screen (0.5-1.5s). Bonuses can appear 2 times at most, each.

How to play

  • Choose the grid size: 4x4, 5x5 or 6x6.
  • Press START - the timer begins at 30s.
  • Click the positive icons (exams and bonuses), avoid negative ones.
  • Score and timer update live.
  • The game has sounds for hits, mistakes and bonuses.

Advice

  • Don't click blindly - "bad" icons remove points or reset the score.
  • The bonuses are rare - try to grab them.
  • Bigger grids have more targets but are harder to track.
